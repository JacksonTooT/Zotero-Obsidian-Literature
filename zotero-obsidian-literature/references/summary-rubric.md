# Scientific summary rubric

Extract the paper's argument rather than paraphrasing only the abstract.

1. **Scientific question**: the unresolved phenomenon, relationship, mechanism, prediction, or methodological gap being tested.
2. **Datasets**: named datasets, study regions/populations, instruments, simulations, sample sizes, temporal coverage, or experimental material. Do not classify software as a dataset.
3. **Methods**: study design, preprocessing, statistical/modeling methods, experiments, validation, and comparisons. Prefer specific method names.
4. **Findings**: results actually supported by the paper. Preserve direction, conditions, and important quantitative effects when available.
5. **Problem solved**: explain the contribution relative to the stated gap; do not simply repeat the findings.
6. **Limitations**: author-stated limitations first, then clearly labeled scope restrictions supported by the text.

Evidence priority: full text > abstract > metadata. If full text is truncated or unavailable, say so. Never infer a dataset or method solely from the title.
